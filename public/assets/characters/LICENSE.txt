Downloaded 2026-10-16. Repacked from self-contained glTF JSON into GLB;
mesh, skinning, materials and all 24 authored animation clips are unchanged.
The game clones the model, recolors uniform cloth for teams, attaches an
original procedural weapon to the wrist, and blends masked animation clips.
No Mixamo or Counter-Strike model is included in this asset.

Verified animation names:
Death, Gun_Shoot, HitRecieve, HitRecieve_2, Idle, Idle_Gun,
Idle_Gun_Pointing, Idle_Gun_Shoot, Idle_Neutral, Idle_Sword, Interact,
Kick_Left, Kick_Right, Punch_Left, Punch_Right, Roll, Run, Run_Back,
Run_Left, Run_Right, Run_Shoot, Sword_Slash, Walk, Wave.

There is no authored reload clip. Reload handling in the game combines
the Interact arm motion with procedural weapon motion.
